License Permit Registration

STEP 1
Gather Your Documents

Collect documents like your passport, birth certificate, and driver’s license to reach six points.

STEP 2
Verify Your Identity

Present your collected documents at the verification office. Ensure that your information matches official records.

STEP 3
Submit Required Forms

Fill out the necessary forms completely and accurately. Double-check for any missing information before submission.

STEP 4
Receive Confirmation

Once your application is processed, you will receive a confirmation notice via email or mail.
