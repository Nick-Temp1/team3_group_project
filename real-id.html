Book a Real ID Appointment

What is a Real ID? A Real ID is a federally compliant form of identification required to board domestic flights and access certain federal facilities. It is marked with a gold or black star in the upper portion of the card.

Starting May 7, 2025, a Real ID-compliant license or ID is required to fly domestically within the United States. An appointment is required to obtain your Real ID at a DMV agency.

What to Bring: To receive a Real ID, you must provide documentation proving your identity, Social Security number, and two proofs of your current address. Visit the 6 Points of ID page for a full list of acceptable documents.

All documents must be original or certified copies. Photocopies and digital versions are not accepted.
