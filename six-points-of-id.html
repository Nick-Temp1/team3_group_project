Six Points of ID

What is the Six Points of ID?

The Six Points of ID system is used to verify your identity. Different documents are assigned point values, and you must provide documents that total at least six points.

Accepted Documents

  • Passport
  • Birth Certificate
  • Driver’s License
  • Social Security Card
  • Utility Bill

Example Documents

  • Passport - 5 Points
  • Birth Certificate - 4 Points
  • Driver’s License - 3 Points
  • Social Security Card - 2 Points
  • Utility Bill - 1 Point
