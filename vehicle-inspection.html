Vehicle Inspection

Basic (non-commercial) Vehicles: Vehicles need to be inspected once every two years with the exception of new vehicles. New vehicles need a five-year inspection. To determine when your vehicle is due for inspection, check the inspection sticker on your windshield.

You may complete your inspection up to two months before the expiration date.

Commercial Vehicles: Commercial vehicles typically require more frequent inspections due to higher usage and safety requirements. These vehicles are often inspected annually to ensure they meet safety and operational standards.

Inspection requirements for commercial vehicles may vary depending on the vehicle's size, weight, and how it is used.
