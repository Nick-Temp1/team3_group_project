Book a Road Test Appointment

Road Test Appointment: A road test is required to obtain your driver license. You must hold a valid permit for the required period before scheduling your road test. Make sure you arrive with a licensed driver and a vehicle that passes a pre-inspection.

Appointments are available Monday through Saturday at participating DMV locations. You may reschedule or cancel up to 48 hours before your appointment without penalty.

Before You Book: Ensure you meet all eligibility requirements. You must be at least 17 years old, have held a valid permit for the required time, and have completed any required supervised driving hours.

On the day of your test, bring your valid permit, a completed 6 Points of ID, and a vehicle with current registration and insurance.


Road Test — Common Questions

You must bring your valid learner's permit, proof of identity (6 Points of ID), and a vehicle with current registration and insurance. The accompanying licensed driver must also have their license present.

Most applicants must hold their permit for a minimum of one year before scheduling a road test. If you are under 21, you must also complete the required supervised driving hours.

If you do not pass your road test, you may reschedule after a waiting period. There is no limit on the number of attempts, but each re-test requires a new appointment and applicable fees.
