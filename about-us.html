Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule a DMV appointment?

Schedule online through our website or call our customer service. Have your ID and vehicle info ready.

What documents are required for license renewal?

Current driver’s license, proof of identity, proof of residency, and sometimes Social Security card. Check online for specifics.

How do I register a new vehicle?

Bring vehicle title, insurance, completed registration form, and payment. Register online or at a DMV office.
